2. Description of the Service

Dental Monitoring Dashboard is an operational productivity and workflow analytics platform designed for dental monitoring specialists and clinical administrators. The platform provides:

  • Automated PDF Ingestion: Connects to a designated folder in your Google Drive to detect and parse exported daily monitoring productivity reports.
  • Performance Telemetry: Aggregates operational case counts, 2D and 3D case distributions, hourly throughput, and shift scoring metrics.
  • Earnings & Compensation Estimations: Calculates expected operational earnings based on configured benchmark rates (such as 1.65 MAD for 2D cases and 2.75 MAD for 3D cases).
  • Daily Notes: Allows operators to record operational annotations and shift observations tied to specific calendar report dates.
  • Vector PDF Reports: Generates downloadable high-resolution vector PDF summaries of daily and billing cycle metrics.
