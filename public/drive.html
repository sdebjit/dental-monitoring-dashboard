Data Governance & Terms

This site is built exclusively to track user productivity at Dental Monitoring. No clinical data is involved—it is an operational dashboard to keep all your tracking in one place.

All Reports Remain in Your Google Drive

All original PDFs remain safely in your own Google Drive. Reports are processed on-the-fly and automatically organized into an archive subfolder—never copied to external file servers or deleted from Drive.

Strict Confidentiality & Zero AI Training

Your productivity metrics are strictly isolated to your authenticated account. We never sell, monetize, share, rent, or train public AI models on your operational data.

Complete Self-Service Data Deletion

You retain complete data ownership. You can permanently purge all stored metrics, reports, active sessions, and OAuth tokens anytime from Settings with one click, leaving your Google Drive completely untouched.
